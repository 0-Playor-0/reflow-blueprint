The idea

What got pasted in at setup — Ideation parses this against the rubric

A four-stage tool that takes a hackathon team from idea to judging: score the idea against the real rubric, split work by real GitHub skill signals, rehearse judge questions grounded in the actual codebase, and export an activity log — all hanging off one shared team session instead of four disconnected tools.
